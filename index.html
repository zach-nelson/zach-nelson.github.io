About

I work primarily as a Senior Geospatial Data Scientist and Science Coordinator responsible for overseeing science relating to the Long-Term Water Agreement between Inyo County and the City of Los Angeles.

I’m a GIS expert, remote sensing specialist, small unmanned aerial system pilot in command, and wildlife habitat ecologist, experienced in statistical, grid-based, and agent-based modeling.

I have a special interest in literate and functional programming, reproducible and open science, automated reporting, data pipelining (targets), and application development (R Shiny and ArcGIS Online mapping applications).

  • 14 yrs Groundwater-dependent ecosystems
  • 24 yrs Avian habitat ecology
  • 22 yrs Remote sensing & mapping

Role & duties

Senior Scientist, Inyo County Water Department — research, monitoring, geospatial systems, staff supervision, and representation before governing bodies.

  • Direct research and monitoring related to riparian and groundwater-dependent ecosystems, wildlife ecology, geospatial applications, and remote sensing
  • Develop and lead interdisciplinary research projects that improve monitoring and management to protect or enhance the environment
  • Coordinate with senior scientific and management staff to determine and implement Owens Valley management goals and strategies
  • Collect and analyze field and laboratory data; prepare and deliver written and oral technical reports
  • Supervise and train department field staff; conduct annual performance evaluations
  • Prepare staffing and budgets for activities and studies; assist in preparation and management of departmental budgets
  • Participate in department program development; obtain funding; manage grants and contracts related to monitoring and research
  • Develop and manage departmental work plans and grants
  • Represent the department before the Board of Supervisors, the Water Commission, the Standing Committee, the Technical Group, other governmental bodies, private organizations, and the public
